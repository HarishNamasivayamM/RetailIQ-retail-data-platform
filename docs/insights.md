# Verified sample-data insights

These findings are calculated by `retailiq.analytics.metrics` from the generated
100,000-row synthetic dataset (`seed=42`). They are illustrative portfolio
findings, not claims about a real retailer.

1. Electronics contributes **74.74% of revenue** ($24.76M) but has only a
   **9.21% margin**, versus Home at **38.89%**. Revenue mix and margin mix should
   be reviewed together before expanding electronics discounting.
2. Laptop Pro 14 is the largest product by revenue at **$9.20M**, or **27.77%
   of total revenue**. This creates concentration risk even though the product
   remains profitable.
3. Smartphone X has the weakest product margin at **4.55%**. Its **$6.45M** of
   revenue makes pricing, cost, and promotion review more valuable than a simple
   volume ranking.
4. Boston leads store revenue at **$4.24M**, while Denver has the highest store
   margin at **17.26%**. The distinction suggests store comparisons should use
   both scale and profitability.
5. The generated customer base has a **100.00% repeat-customer rate** because
   100,000 transactions are distributed across 2,000 customers. This is a
   synthetic benchmark behavior, not a real retention estimate.
6. Revenue grew **0.84%** from 2024 to 2025 in the generated baseline; monthly
   seasonality is modest, so trend interpretation should remain contextual.
